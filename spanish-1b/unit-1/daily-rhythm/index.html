    Unit 1 Daily Rhythm¶

    Same three-beat structure as Spanish 1A: a quick Bell Ringer to start class, a Daily Discussion requiring real interpersonal use of the day's language, and an Exit Ticket to check understanding before students leave.

    Week 1 — Review of Spanish 1A¶

    Day Focus Bell Ringer Daily Discussion Exit Ticket
    1 Welcome back + greetings review Write a full greeting exchange from memory (name, nice to meet you). Find a partner you didn't sit near in 1A and greet them fully in Spanish. Rate 1-4 how confident you feel with greetings, with one sentence why.
    2 Ser review Fill in the correct form of ser for 5 sentences. In pairs, make 3 true sentences about yourselves using ser. Conjugate ser for "nosotros" and use it in a sentence.
    3 Noun gender review Sort 8 review nouns into "el" or "la." Quiz a partner on 5 nouns from last year, guessing el/la. Write el or la for 3 new nouns the teacher provides.
    4 Age and numbers review Write your age as a full Spanish sentence. Ask 3 classmates their age and find the oldest/youngest in your group. Write a sentence giving a family member's age.
    5 Present another person to the class (slideshow) Look over your notes on your assigned partner one final time. Present your partner to the class using a 1-slide "get to know you" slideshow. Write one new thing you learned about a classmate today.

    Week 2 — New Verbs: -Er and -Ir¶

    Day Focus Bell Ringer Daily Discussion Exit Ticket
    6 Intro to -er verbs (comer) Guess the pattern: given "como, comes, come," predict "comemos" and "comen." In pairs, take turns saying what you eat for breakfast using comer. Conjugate comer for "tú."
    7 Intro to -ir verbs (escribir) Compare -er and -ir endings side by side for "yo" and "tú." With a partner, write and read aloud one sentence each using escribir. Conjugate escribir for "ellos."
    8 Mixed -er/-ir practice Sort 6 verbs into "-er" and "-ir" columns. Small groups race to correctly conjugate 5 mixed -er/-ir verbs on a whiteboard. Conjugate one -er and one -ir verb for "nosotros," and note what's different.
    9 Weekend activity vocabulary Match 5 weekend-activity verbs to matching icons. Ask 3 classmates "¿Qué haces los fines de semana?" and record their answers. Write one full sentence about your own weekend activity.
    10 Clarifier phrases (a él, a ellos) Fill in the missing clarifier: "___ le gusta el fútbol" (for "him"). In pairs, describe a third classmate's likes using "a él/a ella le gusta..." Write one sentence using "a ellos les gusta."

    Week 3 — Seasons, Months, and Weather¶

    Day Focus Bell Ringer Daily Discussion Exit Ticket
    11 The four seasons Match 4 season names to matching weather icons. In pairs, name your favorite season and one reason why, in Spanish. Write the Spanish word for the current season.
    12 Weather expressions Fill in "hace ___" for 4 weather pictures (sol, calor, frío, viento). Small groups predict today's real weather using Spanish weather phrases, then check a forecast. Write today's actual weather in one Spanish sentence.
    13 Months of the year Say the months in order aloud with a partner, timing yourselves. Ask a partner "¿Cuál es tu mes favorito?" and why. Write your birthday month in Spanish.
    14 Weather + season combined practice Use the Weather and Season Explorer MicroSim on the Unit 1 content page to review 3 seasons. In pairs, describe the typical weather for each of the 4 seasons in your home state. Write one sentence connecting a season to its typical weather.
    15 Review: verbs + weather + seasons Correct one intentionally wrong sentence the teacher displays (wrong verb ending or season). Small groups build a 4-sentence weekend weather forecast script. Turn in your group's weather forecast script as today's exit ticket.

    Week 4 — Culture, Review, and Unit Wrap-Up¶

    Day Focus Bell Ringer Daily Discussion Exit Ticket
    16 Question word review Match 5 question words (qué, cuándo, con quién, qué tiempo hace, cuál) to sample answers. In pairs, interview each other using at least 3 different question words. Write one question you'd ask a new pen pal about their weekend.
    17 Watch chapter video on Florida Predict one thing you expect to see about Spanish in Florida. Small groups compare Spanish use in Florida to Spanish use in Minnesota. Write one fact you learned about Florida from the video.
    18 Full unit review (stations) Warm up by reviewing your Unit 1 vocabulary list for 3 minutes. Rotate through 4 review stations (verbs, weather, clarifiers, question words) with a small group. Identify the one station you found hardest, to guide tomorrow's review.
    19 Full unit review (game day) Quick recall: list 3 -er/-ir verbs from memory before class starts. Play a whole-class review game (e.g. Blooket) covering all of Unit 1. Write one sentence combining a weekend activity and the weather.
    20 Unit 1 test + Semester I Final Exam Silent independent warm-up: re-read your Unit 1 vocabulary list one final time. No discussion task — test day. The Unit 1 test (and Semester I Final Exam, per the curriculum map) serves as today's exit ticket.
