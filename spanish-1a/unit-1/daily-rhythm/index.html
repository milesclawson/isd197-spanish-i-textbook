    Unit 1 Daily Rhythm¶

    Every class day in this unit follows the same three-beat rhythm:

    1. Bell Ringer (first 3-5 minutes) — a quick, low-stakes warm-up students do the moment they sit down.
    2. Daily Discussion (during class) — a short interpersonal task requiring students to actually speak or write Spanish to a partner, not just about Spanish.
    3. Exit Ticket (last 3-5 minutes) — a fast individual check for understanding before students leave, used to gauge whether the next day needs re-teaching.

    Week 1 — Capítulo Preliminar: Alphabet, Colors, Numbers¶

    Day Focus Bell Ringer Daily Discussion Exit Ticket
    1 Course intro + alphabet Write your first name, then spell it out loud using Spanish letter sounds when called on. In pairs, take turns spelling your partner's name in Spanish. On a notecard, write the Spanish name of one letter that surprised you and why.
    2 Alphabet + silent H, J, LL Circle every silent "h" in a list of 5 words (hola, hermano, historia, hoy, hasta). With a partner, practice saying "hola," "jugo," and "llamo" correctly three times each. Say one word out loud to the teacher (or record it) using the correct sound for h, j, or ll.
    3 Colors Match 5 color swatches to their Spanish names (teacher-provided handout). Point around the room and ask a partner "¿De qué color es esto?" for 4 objects. List the Spanish word for 3 colors you're wearing today.
    4 Numbers 1-15 Count aloud from 1-15 in Spanish along with a partner. In pairs, take turns saying a number 1-15 for your partner to write as a digit. Write the Spanish word for the number of letters in your first name.
    5 Numbers 16-30 + quiz Fill in the missing number in a 16-30 counting sequence. Quiz day — no discussion task; focus is the Capítulo Preliminar quiz. Turn in the Capítulo Preliminar quiz as today's exit ticket.

    Week 2 — Ch.1 Introductions¶

    Day Focus Bell Ringer Daily Discussion Exit Ticket
    6 Me llamo / ¿Cómo te llamas? Translate "My name is ___" into Spanish on paper. Walk the room and introduce yourself to 3 classmates using me llamo. Write how you would ask someone their name.
    7 Mucho gusto / Igualmente Match "Mucho gusto" and "Igualmente" to their English meanings. In pairs, act out a full greeting: introduce yourselves and respond to the introduction. Write the two ways you can respond when someone says "Mucho gusto" to you.
    8 ¿De dónde eres? / Soy de... Fill in the blank: "Yo ___ de Minnesota." Ask 3 classmates "¿De dónde eres?" and record their answers. Write one full sentence saying where you are from.
    9 Combining greetings Unscramble a mixed-up 4-line greeting conversation into the correct order. With a new partner, perform the full greeting conversation from memory (name, nice to meet you, where from). Self-rate 1-4 how confident you feel introducing yourself in Spanish, with one sentence why.
    10 Review + culture (Spain intro) Look at a map of Spain and name one thing you already know about it. Small groups predict 3 things they might see in the Spain chapter video. Write one question you have about Spain before watching the video tomorrow.

    Week 3 — Ser, Noun Gender, Age, and Date¶

    Day Focus Bell Ringer Daily Discussion Exit Ticket
    11 The verb ser Fill in "soy, eres, es, somos, son" to complete 5 short sentences. In pairs, take turns making true sentences about yourselves using ser (soy estudiante, soy de..., etc.). Conjugate ser for "ellos" and use it in one sentence.
    12 Noun gender (-o/-a) Sort 8 nouns into "el" or "la" columns based on their ending. With a partner, quiz each other on 5 vocabulary words, guessing "el" or "la" for each. Write "el" or "la" for 3 new nouns the teacher provides.
    13 Tengo ___ años Write your own age as a full sentence: "Tengo ___ años." Ask 3 classmates their age and note who is oldest/youngest in your group. Write a sentence giving the age of a family member.
    14 Giving the date Write today's date in Spanish. In pairs, quiz each other on 3 random dates ("¿Cuál es la fecha?"). Write next Friday's date in a complete Spanish sentence.
    15 Review: ser + gender + age + date Correct one intentionally wrong sentence the teacher displays (mixed-up gender or verb form). Small groups build a 4-sentence self-introduction using everything from this week. Turn in your group's 4-sentence introduction as today's exit ticket.

    Week 4 — Vocabulary, Culture, and Unit Wrap-Up¶

    Day Focus Bell Ringer Daily Discussion Exit Ticket
    16 Sports and music vocabulary Circle the sports/music words you recognize in a short word list. Poll 3 classmates: "¿Qué deporte te gusta?" and "¿Qué música te gusta?" List 2 sports and 2 music genres in Spanish.
    17 Classes and food vocabulary Match 5 class names to matching school-supply icons. In pairs, share your class schedule and favorite food using Spanish vocabulary. Write the Spanish name for your favorite class.
    18 Watch chapter video on Spain Predict one custom or tradition you expect to see in the video. Small groups compare one thing from the video to something in their own community. Write one fact you learned about Spain from the video.
    19 Full unit review (game day) Warm up with 5 rapid-fire vocabulary flashcards (see the Vocabulary Flashcard Sprint MicroSim on the Unit 1 content page). Play a whole-class review game (e.g. Blooket) covering all of Unit 1. Identify the one Unit 1 topic you feel least confident about, to guide tomorrow's review.
    20 Unit 1 test Silent independent warm-up: re-read your Unit 1 vocabulary list one final time. No discussion task — test day. The Unit 1 test itself serves as today's exit ticket.
