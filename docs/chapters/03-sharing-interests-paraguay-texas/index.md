# Sharing Interests and Comparing Cultures: Paraguay and Texas

## Summary

Building on personal identity from the previous chapter, students learn to express likes and dislikes with *gustar* and indirect object pronouns, describe favorite activities and hobbies, and compare greeting customs and cultural norms across Paraguay, Texas, and their own community. This closes out Unidad 1.
